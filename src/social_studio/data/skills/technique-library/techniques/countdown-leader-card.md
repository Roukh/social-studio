# countdown-leader-card

**Family:** texture, type. **Wants:** 1.5-2.5 s. **Source:** prompt-motion.com's gallery (lexnlin-6161a6),
studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

A giant blocky numeral readout (a time, a count) sits over a rough, textured ground (brick, concrete, film
grain), styled like an old cinema countdown leader or a worksite sign rather than a clean digital display. A
thin status line above it reads like a build log (\"rebuilding, n targets\"). It marks a section as "in
progress" with analogue, physical weight instead of a slick UI counter.

## Build

- The numeral is set in a heavy slab or grotesk face, large enough to fill most of the card, over a tiled
  texture image (brick/concrete) at full saturation so it reads as a physical surface, not a gradient.
- The numeral ticks on a coarse interval (whole seconds, not smooth tweening) to read as a worksite clock
  rather than an animated UI, each tick a hard cut with no easing.
- A thin progress rule at the bottom of the card, with a single marker sliding along it, places this card in a
  longer sequence of similar cards.

## Sound

A mechanical tick on each coarse step, a low ambient rumble under the hold.

## Adapting

Use for a "building/working" beat that wants to feel physical and unglamorous rather than slick — the opposite
mood from a glass progress ring.
