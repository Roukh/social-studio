# riso-print-flicker

**Family:** texture, type. **Wants:** 2-3 s for the full cycle. **Source:** rneayan-474bcf
(prompt-motion.com/rneayan-474bcf), studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

The same short headline flashes through four or five different two-colour ink combinations in rapid
succession, as if a riso press were recalibrating its registration, before settling on the final colourway and
holding.

## Build

- One DOM card with a textured background (a diagonal stripe or halftone dot fill) and a headline on top.
- Hard-swap, never crossfade, both the background's two spot colours and the type's ink colour together, every
  0.15-0.25 s, cycling through a short fixed palette list.
- Hold the final combination at least three times as long as any one flicker step, so the eye lands on it.

## Sound

A fast paper-flutter or shutter-click rattle through the flicker, landing on a single thunk as it settles.

## Adapting

Use for any brand with a print or craft texture as a hook device. The headline is always the brief's own claim,
never the technique's own words.
