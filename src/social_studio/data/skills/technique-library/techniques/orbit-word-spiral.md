# orbit-word-spiral

**Family:** type, structure. **Wants:** 2-3 s. **Source:** an "Overthinking" motion study in
prompt-motion.com's gallery (prompt-motion.com/gizakdag-cf4ae6), studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

Several short words or phrases sit on curved baselines around a set of concentric rings, each word at its own
radius, rotation and size, overlapping as they land so no single line of thought is ever legible alone, only
the mass of them. A faint geometric scaffold (concentric circles, a rotated square or diamond grid) sits
behind them, reinforcing the orbit.

## Build

- The scaffold is a set of static or very slowly rotating SVG circles and a rotated square grid, low opacity.
- Each word is set along an SVG `textPath` on its own invisible circular or elliptical arc at a unique radius;
  its arc's rotation offset and font size are seeded per word.
- Words fade/scale in on a loose, overlapping stagger (not a clean beat grid) so they read as simultaneous
  rather than sequential, each landing at a slightly different time within a short window.

## Sound

A low anxious drone or tremolo bed, with a soft flutter per word landing, overlapping rather than discrete.

## Adapting

Use it for any idea that is about many competing thoughts, options or voices at once rather than one message:
indecision, brainstorming chaos, a wall of customer feedback, option paralysis.
