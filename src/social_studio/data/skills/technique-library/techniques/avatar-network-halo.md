# avatar-network-halo

**Family:** data, particles. **Wants:** 2-2.5 s. **Source:** a dub.co launch promo shared by @steventey
(x.com/steventey/status/2103625902211088880), studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

A loose cloud of small round avatar or icon bubbles scatters across the frame at varying depths and sizes
around a single glowing hub mark at the centre. No lines connect them; the implied network reads from
proximity and a shared radial glow instead. Beneath it a dollar or count value rolls up live.

## Build

- The hub is a bright circular mark with a soft bloom, pinned centre-frame.
- Each avatar bubble sits at a seeded position within a radial falloff from the centre (closer bubbles larger
  and brighter, farther ones smaller and dimmer), landing on a loose, overlapping stagger rather than a clean
  beat grid.
- The value beneath uses `data-infographic.md`'s digit roll-up, synced to finish just after the bubbles settle.

## Sound

A soft shimmer as the bubbles populate, and a rising tone under the counting value.

## Adapting

Use it for any claim about a network effect: partners, referrals, community members, connected accounts. Swap
the avatars for the right kind of node (people, logos, devices) for the claim being made.
