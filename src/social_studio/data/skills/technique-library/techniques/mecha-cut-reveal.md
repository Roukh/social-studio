# mecha-cut-reveal

**Family:** type, reveal. **Wants:** 1.5-2.5 s per unit, then a 0.5-1 s burst. **Source:** an anime
mecha-combine film in prompt-motion.com's gallery, studied 2026-10-07.

## Looks

A hard cut per unit (a part, a feature, a team member), each on its own full-bleed card with a colour-coded
label chip that holds for a beat. It ends in a flash and a burst title that brings the units together.

## Build

- One flat-shaded SVG or canvas illustration per card. Hard swaps on a stepped index, with no crossfades.
- The label chip reuses the HUD's mono label style. The burst title reuses `wordmark-assemble.md`'s slam,
  over a radial flash and speed lines.

## Sound

A hit on each cut, a riser through the last unit, and a big hit on the burst.

## Adapting

Use it for anything that combines into a whole: the parts of a service, the steps of a process, the features
of a product.
