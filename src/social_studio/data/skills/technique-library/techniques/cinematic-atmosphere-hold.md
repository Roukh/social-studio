# cinematic-atmosphere-hold

**Family:** texture, establishing or close. **Wants:** 2-3 s. **Source:** a cinematic film ("a pelican riding a
bicycle") in prompt-motion.com's gallery, studied 2026-10-07.

## Looks

A near-still, painterly frame carried by atmosphere: motes or small shapes drifting at different depths, a
warm grade, and one flare at the brightest point. It is calm, but never empty.

## Build

- A static or barely panning background layer (gradients, shapes or a generative field).
- Two to four sprite layers drift at different speeds as functions of `t`, for parallax.
- A grade: a coloured overlay with `mix-blend-mode`, plus the `grain-vignette.md` pass.
- One bloom sprite pinned to the brightest point, pulsing slowly.

## Sound

A low pad and room tone, with no hits.

## Adapting

Use it as a breath between busy scenes, an opening establishing shot, or the settle before the end card.
