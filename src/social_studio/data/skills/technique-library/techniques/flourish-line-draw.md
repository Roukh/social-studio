# flourish-line-draw

**Family:** type, texture. **Wants:** 1.5-2.5 s. **Source:** prompt-motion.com's gallery (lexnlin-038035),
studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

A single continuous pen line draws itself across the frame in dense, looping calligraphic flourishes, the way
a hand might warm up before writing, never resolving into legible letters. It fills open space with craft and
motion between two type beats, reading as texture and transition at once.

## Build

- One (or a few) long SVG path with many control points, drawn with `strokeDashoffset` from full length to 0
  over 1.5-2.5s on a steady linear-ish ease (slight ease is fine, but it should feel like one continuous
  gesture, not a series of separate strokes).
- Keep the stroke thin and monochrome, matching the body type's colour, so it reads as handwriting practice
  rather than a decorative border.

## Sound

A very soft pen-on-paper scratch, barely audible, rising and falling with the line's speed.

## Adapting

Good as connective tissue between two type-led beats, or as a texture layer behind a word. Don't let it last
longer than the eye needs to register "a hand is at work here."
