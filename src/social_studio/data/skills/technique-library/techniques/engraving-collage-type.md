# engraving-collage-type

**Family:** texture, collage, type. **Wants:** 1.5-3 s. **Source:** prompt-motion.com's gallery
(lexnlin-038035), studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

On a warm off-white paper ground, a scrap of antique engraving or etching (a scientific diagram, a study
sketch, a geometric solid) sits beside a short line of elegant serif display type, with one small accent-colour
dot threading between them like a pin on a specimen board. The pairing reads as considered and literary, not
decorative for its own sake: the image is evidence, the type is the claim.

## Build

- The engraving image is desaturated to near-monochrome (or genuinely black-and-white) so it reads as period
  artwork against the warm paper ground; it never gets a drop shadow or a frame, just placed directly on the
  page.
- The accent dot is a small filled circle, placed where the eye would naturally travel between image and type
  (on a flourish's end, at a letter's dot); it can pulse very subtly (`scale 1 -> 1.05`, slow) to stay alive
  without calling attention to itself.
- Type and image arrive with a plain, fast fade/lift (`y: 6-10px -> 0`, 200-300ms) — the craft is in the
  pairing and the ground, not in a flashy entrance.

## Sound

Near silence or a very soft paper-like texture; this family of shots carries mood through restraint.

## Adapting

Source only public-domain or genuinely owned period artwork. In 9:16, stack the image above the type instead
of beside it, keeping generous paper margin on all sides.
