# vintage-title-card

**Family:** type, open. **Wants:** 3-8 s. **Source:** a shadow-play explainer studied from
x.com/LLMJunky/status/2106411941497389342, studied 2026-10-09.

## Looks

A title letters in with an ornate, period display face, gold against near-black, each letter carrying a soft
outer glow like old marquee lighting. A short subtitle in a smaller serif, in quotes or italics, settles
underneath a beat later, with a fine hairline rule or small flourish separating the two. The whole card reads
like the opening card of a silent film or a stage play's programme, not a digital product.

## Build

- The title is DOM or SVG text in a decorative display face; each letter's glow is a blurred duplicate layer
  behind it, low opacity, warm colour.
- The title fades/rises in as one block or a very loose stagger (0.02-0.04 s per letter, far looser than
  `wordmark-assemble.md`), `power2.out`.
- The subtitle fades in after a pause at lower contrast, with a thin rule or small ornamental glyph between
  title and subtitle.
- A very fine film-grain and a soft vignette sit over the whole card, see `grain-vignette.md`.

## Sound

A low orchestral swell or a single warm chord under the title's arrival, a soft hiss of period film grain
under the hold.

## Adapting

Use it for any film reaching for a period, literary or theatrical register: a documentary, a brand with
heritage, a story-led piece. Keep the display face consistent with any chapter cards later in the same film.
