## Question

### Principles, with numbers

1. **Disney's 12 principles** (Johnston & Thomas, *Illusion of Life*, 1981): squash & stretch, anticipation, staging, straight-ahead/pose-to-pose, follow-through & overlap, slow-in/slow-out, arcs, secondary action, timing, exaggeration, solid drawing, appeal. [uxdesign.cc](https://uxdesign.cc/how-disney-conquered-the-animation-industry-with-these-12-principles-687acca99716) · [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/animation/discover/principles-of-animation.html)
2. **Easing curves** (GSAP): Power2≈cubic, Power3≈quart; back eases overshoot w/ strength, e.g. `back.out(1.7)`; elastic eases take amplitude+period, e.g. `elastic.out(1, 0.3)`. [GSAP Eases](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Eases)
3. **Spring physics**: stiffness 100–200 soft, 300–400 standard UI, 500+ snappy; damping 10–15 bouncy, 20–25 balanced, 30–40 subtle, 50+ no bounce. Framer Motion default: stiffness 100/damping 10/mass 1; react-spring: stiffness 170/damping 26. [maximeheckel.com](https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/the-physics-behind-spring-animations/) · [react-motion](https://tessl.io/registry/tessl/npm-react-motion/0.5.0/files/docs/spring-system.md)
4. **Duration tokens**: Apple HIG keeps UI animation 0.25–1.0s (degrades under Reduce Motion). IBM Carbon: "productive"=cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.38,0.9), "expressive"=cubic-bezier(0.4,0.14,0.3,1); duration scales w/ distance/size. Material 3: named duration/easing tokens. [Apple HIG](https://developer-mdn.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/foundations/motion) · [Carbon motion](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/motion/overview/) · [Material 3](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/easing-and-duration/tokens-specs)
5. **Stagger** (GSAP): `stagger.each` = fixed sec-gap/child (e.g. 0.1); `stagger.amount` splits total duration across children; `from` sets emanation point (start/center/edges/random/end/index); `grid:"auto"` drives 2D "wave" staggers. [GSAP Staggers](https://greensock.com/docs/v3/Staggers)
6. **Kinetic typography**: ~3 words/sec legibility ceiling; important words held ≥3s; adult reading ~300wpm (~5 words/sec, 0.2s/word) is a floor not target; explainer frames hold 3–7 words. Ad hierarchy: hook=fastest, benefit=medium, claim=pause, CTA=longest. [nemovideo.com](https://www.nemovideo.com/blog/kinetic-typography-product-ads)
7. **Cutting on the beat**: interval = `60/(BPM/beat-multiple)`; 2 beats@120 BPM=1.0s/cut; in frames `(60/BPM)*fps` per beat (120 BPM@30fps=15 frames=0.5s/beat). Vary vs. beat-grid (2/4/8/16-bar holds) to avoid mechanical reads. One vendor: beat-aligned cuts tie to +23% avg watch time (unverified figure). [nofilmschool](https://nofilmschool.com/2017/06/watch-rhythmic-editing) · [beatsyncpro.ai](https://beatsyncpro.ai/blog/beat-synced-video-clips-free-download-pillar.html)
8. **Motion blur/shutter**: 180° rule sets exposure ≈1/(2×fps) — 1/48@24fps, 1/60@30fps; shutter angle = same in degrees (180°=50% exposed, 90°=25%). A default, not law. [diyphotography.net](https://www.diyphotography.net/?p=309010) · [provideocoalition](https://provideocoalition.com/the-180-shutter-angle-rule-is-broken)
9. **Metaball/gooey**: overlap soft shapes, `feGaussianBlur` to fuse, `feColorMatrix` to threshold alpha to a hard edge (SVG filter); CSS equiv. `filter: blur() contrast()`. [freefrontend.com](https://freefrontend.com/javascript-gooey/) · [dev.to](https://dev.to/antogarand/svg-metaballs-35pj)
10. **Particle flow fields**: sample a 2D (Perlin/custom) vector field at each particle's position, normalize direction, step along it each frame; Canvas2D/p5.js/three.js for GPU scale. [sighack.com](https://sighack.com/post/getting-creative-with-perlin-noise-fields) · [clicktorelease.com](https://clicktorelease.com/code/generative-lines-flow-fields)
11. **Variable fonts**: `wght` axis (typically 100–900) + other axes (width/slant/italic/optical size) interpolate continuously; CSS `font-variation-settings` is directly animatable. [Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/lt/after-effects/desktop/variable-font-axes/work-with-variable-font-axes/variable-font-axes-support.html)
12. **FUI/HUD language** (Ash Thorp, Jayse Hansen et al.): corner brackets, scanlines, readouts, progress ticks as storytelling props over plausible real UI. [hudsandguis.com](https://www.hudsandguis.com/home/2016/11/29/cod-infinity-warfare-ui) · [madebyloop.co.uk](https://madebyloop.co.uk/blog/examples-of-great-sci-fi-interfaces-in-films)
13. **Sound/loudness for social**: ~-14 LUFS integrated is the practical normalization target (some sources: Instagram/TikTok nearer -10 to -12 LUFS); unnormalized exports risk platform attenuation vs. adjacent content. [openclip.app](https://openclip.app/learn/lufs) · [opus.pro](https://opus.pro/blog/best-loudness-normalizers)
14. **Social hook and safe zones**: first ~1.5–3s decide watch-through; open on payoff not logo. 9:16 @1080×1920 shared canvas. Unified (March 2026) Reels/Stories safe zone: top ~14%, bottom ~20–35%, ~6% each side. Pacing: a visual change every 1.5–3s. [tigerpistol.com](https://tigerpistol.com/vertical-video-ad-best-practices/) · [opus.pro](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length)
15. **Tells of cheap/template motion**: uniform linear easing everywhere; same duration on unrelated elements; "fade everything" as the only transition; centered-subject-on-gradient, no staging/hierarchy; generic particle overlays unmotivated by content; trendy effect over clear communication. [glama.ai](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@endlessblink/clean-cut-mcp/blob/13ec46d11f1e6e5f4987cd7f637c23abda34564d/docs/main-docs/MY-ANIMATION-PROBLEMS.md) · [playbooks.com](https://playbooks.com/skills/dylantarre/animation-principles/slow-in-out-mastery)
